What is “Let Me Prompt That For You”?

I Prompted It For You is Let Me Google That For You, rebuilt for AI chatbots. Everybody has that one friend, coworker or family member who sends you a question that ChatGPT could have answered in three seconds. Instead of answering, you type their question here, copy the “let me prompt that for you” link and send it back.

When they open it, they watch a cursor glide into a chat box, type their exact question letter by letter, and click straight into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Copilot, Google AI Mode or DeepSeek. The answer is waiting. The lesson sticks. Nobody gets hurt (much).

How to make a “let me ChatGPT that for you” link

  1. Type the question. Paste whatever your friend asked you into the big box above.
  2. Pick the AI. Choose ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any other chat the link should open in, or let them pick.
  3. Create and copy the link. One click copies it. On your phone you can share it straight to any app.
  4. Send it. Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage, email, the family group chat. Then sit back.

Why send a prompt link instead of the answer?

Because asking AI is a skill, and the fastest way to learn it is to see it done once. LMGTFY taught a generation that search engines exist. A prompt link teaches the next one that a chatbot can draft that email, explain that error message, plan that trip or settle that argument. It is a gentle nudge, a running joke and a tiny tutorial in one.

It also saves you time. You do not have to read, summarize and retype an answer you would have looked up anyway. You hand over the question and the tool, and they get an answer they can follow up on themselves.

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and more

  • ChatGPT: opens chatgpt.com with the prompt pre-filled. The classic “let me ChatGPT that for you”.
  • Claude: starts a new chat on claude.ai with your question.
  • Perplexity: runs the prompt as a cited answer search.
  • Google AI Mode: asks Google's AI search directly. LMGTFY, all grown up.
  • Grok and Microsoft Copilot: prompt pre-filled.
  • Gemini and DeepSeek: open in a new tab with the prompt copied to the clipboard, ready to paste.

Frequently asked questions

Is this like LMGTFY (Let Me Google That For You)?

Yes, that is exactly the idea. LMGTFY made a search, this makes a prompt. It is the same friendly sarcasm, updated for people who should really be asking an AI assistant.

Is I Prompted It For You free?

Completely free, no sign-up, no ads in your face, and the code is open source on GitHub.

Do you store or read my prompts?

No. The prompt is stored only inside the link you share. There is no server, no database and no analytics cookie. If you do not share the link, nobody sees the prompt.

Why didn't Gemini or DeepSeek open with my prompt?

Some AI chats do not support pre-filling a prompt from a link. For those, we copy the prompt to your clipboard and open the chat in a new tab, so all you have to do is paste.

How long can my prompt be?

Up to 2,000 characters, so the link stays short enough to work in every messenger and browser.

Is it rude to send a “let me AI that for you” link?

It is cheeky, not cruel. Send it to people who will laugh. For your boss, maybe just answer the question.
